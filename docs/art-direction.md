# Art direction — The familiar, made wonderful

Warm ivory, forest green, apricot, muted sage, butter yellow, and antique gold. Tactile clay and painted wood hero artwork; simple dimensional vector illustrations for game mechanics. Editorial serif headings and friendly readable interface type. No neon, casino imagery, generic purple dashboard, or infantilising cartoon people.

Motion should communicate: tiles unfold to reveal an answer, bars grow to reveal votes, score rows arrive in sequence, and a short paper-confetti celebration marks the ending. Ambient motion is slow and restrained. Sound is optional, quiet, and locally synthesised. All non-essential motion respects reduced-motion settings.
